# Handwritten Advice

*by Śrīla Bhakti Sundar Govinda Dev-Goswāmī Mahārāj*

![Handwritten advice photo](https://github.com/scsm-ua/songbook-resources/blob/main/images/en-2026/2.png?raw=true)

</div>

1. Read Śrī Gītā and Holy Books regularly
2. Congregational chanting arrangement
3. Not to criticise anyone
4. Think seriously for spiritual life
5. Avoid Vaiṣṇava aparādh
6. Follow 4 directives
7. Be humble\
   be tolerant\
   give honour to others

Try to increase faith of others in the line of
Mahāprabhu as directed by Śrīla Guru Mahārāj
