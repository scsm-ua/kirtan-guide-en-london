# About This Project

This is the web edition of the **Kīrtan Guide**, Twelfth Edition, published by Sri Chaitanya Saraswat Math, London, in 2026.

## Helpful Links and Tips

Pronunciation guides, recordings of the melodies, and details of the printed book and PDF:

[scsmath.com/KirtanGuide](https://scsmath.com/KirtanGuide/)

## Contribute

The songbook and the web app are developed openly on GitHub. If you notice a mistake or have an idea for an improvement, please feel free to contribute and participate:

- [github.com/scsm-ua/kirtan-guide-en-london](https://github.com/scsm-ua/kirtan-guide-en-london) – songbook texts: the songs and pages of this book
- [github.com/scsm-ua/kirtan-next](https://github.com/scsm-ua/kirtan-next/) – web app code: the website itself
