# Śrī-Śrī-Guru-Gaurāṅga-Rādhā-Gopīnāthjīu

## Hāpāniyā, Śrī Chaitanya Sāraswat Āśram

    śrīmān rāsa-rasārambhī vaṁśī-vaṭa-taṭa-sthitaḥ
    karṣan veṇu-svanair gopīr gopīnāthaḥ śriye ’stu naḥ

*(Śrī Chaitanya-charitāmṛta: Ādi-līlā, 1.17)*

Śrī Gopīnāth, who instigates the exchange of devotional mellows, is standing upon the banks of the Yamunā River at the special place known as Vaṁśīvaṭa. There, the sweet tune of His flute is naturally attracting the hearts of the cowherd maidens of Vraja Dhām. May that charming Lord bring us within His auspicious embrace.

*(Śrīla Kṛṣṇadās Kavirāj Goswāmī)*

    vṛndāyai tulasī-devyai priyāyai keśavasya cha
    kṛṣṇa-bhakti-prade devi satyavatyai namo namaḥ

I eternally offer my respects unto Śrī Vṛndā Devī, Śrīmatī Tulasī Mahārāṇī, who is the dearmost object of Lord Keśava’s affection. I bow down before Satyavatī who can bestow pure dedication unto Lord Kṛṣṇa.

    atha natvā mantra-gurūn gurūn bhāgavatārthadān
    vyāsān jagad-gurūn natvā tato jayam udīrayet

Then, let me bow down before all my spiritual masters: the guru who gave me the divine mantra, as well as all those who have taught me the meaning of *Śrīmad Bhāgavatam*. Let me offer my respectful obeisances unto Śrīla Vyāsadev, the spiritual master of the entire universe and the giver of the all-conquering conclusions of *Śrīmad Bhāgavatam*.

    jayaḥ sa-parikara-śrī-śrī-guru-gaurāṅga-
    gāndharvā-govindasundara-pāda-padmānāṁ jayastu!

Let the holy lotus feet of Śrī Gurudev, Śrīman Mahāprabhu, and Śrī Śrī Gāndharvā-Govindasundar, along with Their eternal associates, be victorious over all!
