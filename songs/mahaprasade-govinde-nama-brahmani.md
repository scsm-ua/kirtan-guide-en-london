# Honouring Prasādam

## [Sanskrit]

*The following verses are recited before honouring Prasādam:*

    mahāprasāde govinde nāma-brahmaṇi vaiṣṇave
    svalpa-puṇya-vatāṁ rājan viśvāso naiva yāyate

“O King, one whose fortune is meagre never develops faith in mahāprasād, Govinda, the Name, the Vedas, or the Vaiṣṇavas.”
